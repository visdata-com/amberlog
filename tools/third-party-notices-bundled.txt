pg_query 内嵌的 C 源码 / C sources bundled in the pg_query crate
================================================================================

pg_query 6.2.0 的 crate 元数据只写 MIT，但它随包带着 libpg_query 6.2.2 的 C 源码，
并在构建时编进 Amberlog 的二进制。这些源码各有许可，crate 包里没有附带许可文件，
下面照录各上游的许可原文。
The pg_query 6.2.0 crate declares only MIT in its metadata, but it ships the C
sources of libpg_query 6.2.2 and compiles them into the Amberlog binary. Those
sources carry their own licenses, which the crate package does not include.
Their upstream license texts are reproduced below.
